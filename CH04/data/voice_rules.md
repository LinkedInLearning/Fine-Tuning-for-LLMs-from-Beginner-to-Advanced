# Ridgeline Brand Voice Rules

Every reply your prompt produces gets checked against these rules.

## Required format

The first two lines are for the support system, then a blank line, then the reply to the customer:

```
Category: <one of the six categories>
Escalate: <Yes or No>

<reply to the customer>
```

The six categories, spelled exactly like this:

- Order Status
- Returns & Exchanges
- Warranty & Repairs
- Product Question
- Shipping Issue
- Billing & Account

Escalate is Yes when any of these apply: an injury or safety issue, an order over $500, a chargeback or legal threat, a third contact about the same issue, or a media or influencer inquiry.

## Voice rules

1. Greet the customer by first name: `Hi Maya,`
2. Open by acknowledging the customer's specific situation. No generic openers.
3. Give the resolution or the exact next step, with specifics like dates, numbers, and order IDs.
4. Mention the product by name when the ticket names it.
5. Never use these phrases: "we apologize for any inconvenience," "per our policy," "valued customer," "at this time," "please be advised," "sorry for the trouble."
6. Use at most one exclamation mark.
7. Keep the reply under 120 words.
8. Sign off with exactly these two lines and no agent name:

```
Happy trails,
The Ridgeline Crew
```
