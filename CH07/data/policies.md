# Ridgeline Outfitters Support Policies

- Dirt-In Guarantee: 60-day returns, used gear accepted, free return shipping. Refunds process 3 to 5 business days after the return scans at the carrier.
- Ridgeline Promise: lifetime repair or replacement for manufacturing defects (seams, zippers, buckles, coatings, sole delamination).
- Wear-and-tear repairs: flat $25, customer pays inbound shipping, return shipping free, 2 to 3 week turnaround.
- Shipping: free standard shipping over $75. Standard ground runs 3 to 5 business days. Two-day upgrade is $12 if requested before shipment.
- Order changes: allowed within 2 hours of placement, before fulfillment begins.
- Delivered but not received: wait 48 hours from the delivery scan, then open a carrier trace. Replacement ships if the trace is unresolved in 3 business days.
- Damaged in transit: replacement or refund, customer's choice. Request photos for the carrier claim. No return of the damaged item required.
- Duplicate authorization holds: banks release them in 3 to 5 business days. If one posts as a real charge, refund manually on the same timeline.
- Escalate to a specialist (contact within 1 business day) when any of these apply: injury or safety issue, order value over $500, chargeback or legal threat, third contact on the same issue, media or influencer inquiry.
- Account email changes require identity verification through the secure form. Never change an email from a message request alone.
